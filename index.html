Talk through a recipe the way you would tell it to someone in your kitchen. Kūra transcribes what you said and pulls out the ingredients, steps, timings, tags and allergens as a card. The model drafts it; you correct it inline before it is saved. Typing gives you the same card, minus the microphone.

        
Learn and love to cook — without losing the recipe your grandmother only ever said out loud.

What it does

    

      

        
A cookbook that works offline

        
Saved recipes are cached on the device: search, scale servings up or down, and work through cook-mode checklists with no signal in the kitchen.

      

      

        
A pantry that can see

        
Add items by typing them, or photograph the shelf and let vision identify what is there.

      

      

        
“What can I cook?”

        
Dishes ranked by how much of the recipe your pantry already covers, linked back to the matching recipes you have saved.

      

      

        
Corrections that teach it

        
Every edit you make to a generated card is diffed and stored, so the model gets better at the kind of cooking you actually do.

      

    

  






  

    

      
How it works

      
Voice in, structured recipe out.

    


    

      
01 · RecordAudio captured in the app and uploaded to private storage.

      
02 · TranscribeDeepgram returns a transcript of what you said.

      
03 · StructureGemini turns loose speech into ingredients, steps, timers and tags.

      
04 · ReviewYou edit the card inline. Nothing is saved until you say so.

      
05 · StorePostgres with row-level security, plus a local cache for offline use.

    


    
No API keys ship in the app bundle. The client holds only the public Supabase anon key; the Deepgram and Gemini keys live as Edge Function secrets, and every function verifies the caller’s token before it does any work.
