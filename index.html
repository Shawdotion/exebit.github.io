Practice

      
Four things we do well.

      
We are deliberately narrow. Everything below shares the same underlying skill: getting close to the machine without losing sight of the person using it.

    

    

      

        01
        
Apple platform applications

        
Native iOS, iPadOS and macOS software written in Swift and SwiftUI. We care about launch time, scroll performance, and the small interaction details that separate an app that works from one people keep.

      

      

        02
        
Developer tooling

        
Command-line tools, instrumentation and libraries that shorten the distance between writing code and understanding what it actually did at runtime.

      

      

        03
        
Systems & performance

        
Profiling, rendering and concurrency work — Metal, memory, threading — for the cases where the gap between acceptable and fast is architectural rather than incidental.

      

      

        04
        
Interface research

        
Prototypes exploring how dense information can be presented calmly: what to show, what to withhold, and when a screen should stay quiet.

      

    

  





  

    

      
Work

      
What we are building.

      
We keep a short list on purpose. Each project below is developed and maintained in house; status is stated plainly rather than dressed up.

    


    

      

        

          
Foveal

          In development
        

        

          
A precision capture tool for macOS. Select a region, annotate it, and hand off a legible artifact instead of a cropped screenshot and a paragraph of explanation. Built for design review, bug reports, and documentation that has to survive being read by someone else a month later.

          
macOS 14+ · Swift, SwiftUI, Metal

        

      


      

        

          
Saccade

          Open source
        

        

          
A lightweight instrumentation layer for Swift applications. Structured traces, timing and lifecycle events, plus a local inspector that runs alongside the app so you can watch behaviour as it happens rather than reconstructing it from logs afterwards.

          
Swift package · iOS, macOS

        

      


      

        

          
Penumbra

          Early research
        

        

          
An experiment in ambient interfaces: information that surfaces when it is relevant and gets out of the way when it is not. Currently a prototype we use ourselves while we work out whether the idea holds up outside of a demo.

          
iOS · prototype

        

      

    

  





  

    

      
Approach

      
How we work.

    

    

      

        
01

        

          
Ship less, and finish it.

          
We would rather release one considered feature than five provisional ones. Scope is the largest single lever on quality, and it is the one most teams refuse to pull.

        

      

      

        
02

        

          
The people who design it, build it.

          
There is no handoff here, so nothing is lost in one. Decisions about how something should feel are made by the same people who know what it will cost to implement.

        

      

      

        
03

        

          
Respect the machine and the person.

          
Fast software is a courtesy. So is software that does not demand attention it has not earned — no manufactured urgency, no notifications written to serve us rather than the person receiving them.

        

      

    

  





  

    

      
Studio

      
Two people, on purpose.

      
exebit is an independent software studio founded by Jeffrey Gatling and Yashwant. We design, build and maintain our own products, and occasionally take on outside work where it overlaps with what we are already good at.

      
Staying small keeps the feedback loop short and the software honest. There is no layer between the decision and the consequence, which is uncomfortable in exactly the way that produces better work.
